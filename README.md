Velvet Vault — a one-night, three-heist stealth game. Sneak past patrolling guards and sweeping cameras, crack the prize, grab the side loot, and reach the getaway door before the alarm brings the whole room down on you. Three heists (the Meridian Gallery, the Harbor Bank, the Crown Museum), each one room that fits a single screen: vision cones you have to read, vents to hide in, smoke bombs, EMP pulses and coin tosses to spend, and an alarm meter that decides whether you walk out rich or get caught. WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sneak, Space to dash, hold E to crack the prize, Q for smoke, 2 for EMP, click to toss a coin. No build step, no network: plain static files under game/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
